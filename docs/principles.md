# N19 principles (draft)

- Freedom of opinion and expression, inspired by Article 19 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
- Disagreement or criticism alone is not a moderation violation.
- Equal rules, independent of political viewpoints, popularity or paid membership.
- Platform-assigned public handles; editable, non-unique display names.
- Public source inspection; private user information and production operations.
- Automated detection of sensitive or prohibited material; human review and appeal paths for errors.
- Sensitive media visibility and notifications are to be finalized before launch. Do not silently suppress ordinary political speech.
- No paid ranking advantage.

Applicable legal obligations, reporting, age safeguards and retention policies require review before public registration. These are design principles, not final terms of service.
